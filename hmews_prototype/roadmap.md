# HMEWS Roadmap

1. Digital proof of concept — synthetic longitudinal biomarkers and anomaly detection.
2. Public real-world data — map approved biomarker/clinical datasets into a documented common schema.
3. Multi-modal learning — combine molecular, laboratory and physiological signals.
4. Personalization — model population baseline, individual baseline, rate of change and interactions.
5. Virtual sensor layer — mathematically transform real biomarkers into hypothetical sensor outputs.
6. Clinical validation — prospective research only after rigorous retrospective/external validation and separate safety/regulatory work for any physical sensor.

Metrics: sensitivity, specificity, AUROC/AUPRC, false alerts per patient-month, lead time, calibration, subgroup performance and missing-data robustness.
